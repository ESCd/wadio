1. WHAT WADIO IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Wadio is an internet radio platform consisting of:

  - A web client that lets users discover stations, search, queue tracks,
    and control playback.
  - A Discord bot that joins voice channels and streams internet radio
    stations at the direction of Discord server members.

Wadio aggregates station metadata and stream URLs from the Radio Browser
community directory (radio-browser.info). We do not host, store, own, or
distribute any audio content ourselves. All audio is streamed directly from
independent third-party broadcasters.
