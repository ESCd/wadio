================================================================================
WADIO — PRIVACY POLICY
================================================================================
Effective Date: April 16, 2026
Last Updated:   April 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Escape Developers, LLC ("we," "us," or "our")
handles information in connection with the Wadio service, including the Wadio
Discord bot and web client (collectively, "Wadio" or "the Service").

We have designed Wadio to collect as little data as possible. We have no user
accounts, no behavioral tracking, and no advertising. The sections below are
precise about what is incidentally processed when you use the Service.

This Policy applies only to the hosted Wadio instance at https://wadio.live AND
all it's sub-domains. Self-hosted deployments of Wadio are operated independently
by their respective operators and are not covered by this Policy.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We do not ask you for personal information. We do not create user profiles.
The following data is incidentally processed as part of normal service
operation.

  1a. Web Client

  When you visit or interact with the Wadio web client, our servers
  automatically receive standard HTTP request data, which may include:

    - Your IP address
    - Browser type and version
    - Pages or endpoints visited and timestamps
    - Referring URL (if any)

  This data is retained only in ephemeral server logs — Docker container
  logs on our host systems and an in-memory diagnostics dashboard (Aspire).
  It is not written to a persistent database, not linked to any user
  identity, and is discarded when containers restart or logs rotate. We
  do not currently use any third-party analytics service.

  1b. Discord Bot

  When you interact with the Wadio Discord bot, Discord delivers your
  commands to our bot via their API. As part of processing those commands,
  our bot may incidentally handle:

    - Your Discord user ID and username (as provided by Discord's API)
    - The Discord server (guild) ID and channel ID where the command
      was issued
    - The content of commands you send to the bot

  We log errors and diagnostic events to support bot stability and
  debugging. These logs may incidentally contain guild IDs or command
  context. Logs are stored only in Docker container logs on our servers
  and an in-memory diagnostics dashboard. They are not persisted beyond
  container restarts and are not shared with third parties.

  We do not store a database of commands issued, users who issued them,
  or listening histories.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. COOKIES AND LOCAL STORAGE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking
of any kind.

The Wadio web client may use browser local storage or session cookies solely
for functional purposes, such as remembering your last-selected station or
playback preferences within a session. These are not used to identify you
across sessions or shared with any third party.

If you use a browser with cookies disabled, core functionality of the web
client will continue to work.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES AND DATA PROCESSORS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Operating Wadio requires the involvement of the following third-party
services, each of which may process connection-level data as part of
delivering the Service to you.

  Discord Inc. (discord.com)
    Bot interaction is mediated through Discord's API. Discord collects
    and processes data about your use of their platform under their own
    Privacy Policy (discord.com/privacy). We do not control that
    collection.

  Radio Browser (radio-browser.info)
    We query Radio Browser's API for station metadata and stream URLs.
    Queries originate from our backend servers; your IP address is
    generally not exposed to Radio Browser unless your client queries
    their API directly. Radio Browser is a community-operated,
    open-data project.

  DigitalOcean (digitalocean.com)
    Our servers are hosted on DigitalOcean infrastructure. Ephemeral
    server logs reside on their infrastructure and are subject to their
    Privacy Policy.

  Cloudflare (cloudflare.com)
    Traffic to the Wadio web client passes through Cloudflare for
    routing and security (via Cloudflare Tunnel / Zero Trust). Cloudflare
    may process connection metadata, including IP addresses, in accordance
    with their Privacy Policy. Cloudflare is a US-based company; if you
    are in the EU, this constitutes a transfer of data to a third country.
    Cloudflare participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party for
advertising, marketing, or commercial purposes.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. LEGAL BASIS FOR PROCESSING (EU / EEA USERS — GDPR)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we process the
limited data described above on the following legal basis:

  Legitimate Interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)
    We process IP addresses and ephemeral log data to operate and
    maintain the Service, diagnose errors, and protect against abuse.
    We have assessed that this processing is necessary and proportionate,
    and does not override your fundamental rights, given the strictly
    limited, ephemeral, and non-persistent nature of the data.

We do not process special category data (Article 9 GDPR). We do not use
automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly
significant effects.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Wadio is hosted in the United States. If you access the Service from
outside the United States, your connection data (including IP address) will
be processed on US-based infrastructure.

For EU/EEA users, this constitutes a transfer of personal data to a third
country. We rely on the following transfer mechanisms:

  - Cloudflare: EU-US Data Privacy Framework
  - DigitalOcean: Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

Given that the data we process is limited, ephemeral, and non-persistent,
we assess the risk of this transfer to be low.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA RETENTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

We do not maintain a persistent database of user data. All log data is
ephemeral and tied to the lifecycle of our server processes:

  - Docker container logs are retained on the host system subject to
    Docker's default log rotation settings. Logs are lost on container
    restart unless explicitly persisted (they are not).
  - In-memory diagnostics data (Aspire dashboard) is cleared on process
    restart.

In practice, any incidental data about your use of the Service is
retained for hours to days at most, and is not retrievable after the
relevant process restarts.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. YOUR RIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Depending on where you are located, you may have rights regarding your
personal data. These may include the right to access, correct, restrict
processing of, or delete data we hold about you.

Because we do not maintain persistent user records or link log data to
identifiable individuals, we have limited practical ability to retrieve or
delete data associated with a specific person. We will, however, make
reasonable efforts to respond to any rights request.

To exercise your rights or raise a concern, contact us at:
  sam@escd.dev

If you are in the EU/EEA and believe we have not adequately addressed your
concern, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data
protection authority (supervisory authority).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Wadio is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the
EU/EEA, where a higher minimum applies). We do not knowingly collect
personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the Service
and data has been collected, please contact us and we will take appropriate
steps.
